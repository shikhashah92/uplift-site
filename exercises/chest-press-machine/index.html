Chest Press Machine

A chest exercise on a machine. The chest press machine mainly works your chest, with your triceps and shoulders helping.

  • chest
  • triceps
  • shoulders

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Set the seat so the handles line up with mid-chest.
  • Back and head against the pad, blades pulled back.

How to do it

  1. Press smoothly to near-lockout.
  2. Return under control until the hands are level with the chest.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Seat too low (handles at the neck) or too high (at the belly).
  • Shrugging the shoulders up to press.
  • Letting the stack slam down.
