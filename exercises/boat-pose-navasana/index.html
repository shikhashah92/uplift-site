Boat pose (Navasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Getting into position

  • Sit with knees bent, feet on the floor, hands behind the thighs.

How to do it

  1. Lean back a little and lift the feet, shins parallel to the floor.
  2. Straighten the legs and reach the arms forward if you can.
  3. Chest lifted, spine long.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Rounding the back and collapsing the chest.
