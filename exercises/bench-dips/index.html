Bench Dips

A arms exercise using your body weight. The bench dips mainly works your triceps, with your chest and shoulders helping.

  • triceps
  • chest
  • shoulders

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Hands on the edge of a bench behind you, fingers forward, legs extended out in front, hips close to the bench.

How to do it

  1. Bend the elbows to lower the hips straight down, then press back up to straight arms.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Letting the hips drift forward away from the bench.
  • Shoulders rolling forward into a pinched position.
  • Flaring the elbows out wide.

Muscles worked

The bench dips mainly works your triceps, with your chest and shoulders helping.
