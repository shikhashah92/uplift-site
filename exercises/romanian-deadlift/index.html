Romanian Deadlift

A legs exercise with a barbell. The romanian deadlift mainly works your hamstrings and glutes, with your lower back and forearms helping.

  • hamstrings
  • glutes
  • lower back
  • forearms

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Stand tall holding the bar at the hips, knees softly bent and fixed.
  • Shoulder blades set, bar against the thighs.

How to do it

  1. Push the hips back, sliding the bar down the thighs.
  2. Go until you feel a strong hamstring stretch (~mid-shin), back flat.
  3. Drive the hips forward to stand tall.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Bending the knees more (turns it into a deadlift).
  • Rounding the back / letting the bar drift away from the legs.
  • Over-arching and thrusting the hips at the top.
