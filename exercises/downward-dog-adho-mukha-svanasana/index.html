Downward dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Getting into position

  • Start on hands and knees, hands shoulder-width, fingers spread.

How to do it

  1. Tuck the toes and lift the hips up and back into an upside-down V.
  2. Press the floor away; heels reach toward the mat (knees may bend).
  3. Head between the arms, neck relaxed.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Rounding the back to force the heels down.
  • Shoulders shrugged to the ears.
