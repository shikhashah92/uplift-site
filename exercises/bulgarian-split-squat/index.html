Bulgarian Split Squat

A legs exercise with dumbbells. The bulgarian split squat mainly works your quads and glutes, with your hamstrings and inner thighs helping.

  • quads
  • glutes
  • hamstrings
  • inner thighs

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Rear foot up on a bench behind you, laces down.
  • Front foot far enough forward that the shin stays fairly vertical at the bottom.

How to do it

  1. Lower straight down until the back knee nearly touches the floor.
  2. Drive up through the front heel; most of the work is the front leg.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Front foot too close to the bench (steep shin, knee strain).
  • Pushing off the back leg.
  • Torso collapsing forward.
