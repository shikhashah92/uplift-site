Child’s pose (Balasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Getting into position

  • Kneel, big toes together, knees together or apart.

How to do it

  1. Sit the hips back to the heels and fold forward.
  2. Forehead to the mat; arms forward or alongside the body.
  3. Breathe into the back.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Forcing the hips down with sore knees: put a cushion between.
