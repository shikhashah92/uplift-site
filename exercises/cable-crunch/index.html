Cable Crunch

A core exercise on a cable machine. The cable crunch mainly works your abs, with your obliques helping.

  • abs
  • obliques

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Kneel below a high pulley, rope beside the head / neck.
  • Hips fixed, slight forward hinge.

How to do it

  1. Crunch by rounding the spine, bringing the ribs toward the pelvis.
  2. Squeeze the abs hard; return under control.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Turning it into a hip hinge (bowing from the hips).
  • Pulling with the arms.
  • Letting the weight yank you back upright.
