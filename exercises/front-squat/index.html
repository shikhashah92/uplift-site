Front Squat

A legs exercise with a barbell. The front squat mainly works your quads, with your glutes and abs helping.

  • quads
  • glutes
  • abs

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Bar on the front delts in a clean grip or crossed-arm rack.
  • Elbows high, torso tall, feet about shoulder-width.

How to do it

  1. Descend straight down, keeping the elbows up and torso upright.
  2. Hit depth, then drive up without the chest folding forward.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Elbows dropping so the bar rolls forward.
  • Heels rising off the floor.
  • Rounding the upper back.
