Chair pose (Utkatasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Getting into position

  • Feet together or hip-width; arms reaching up alongside the ears.

How to do it

  1. Bend the knees and sit the hips back as if onto a chair.
  2. Weight in the heels; chest lifted.
  3. Hold, then straighten on an inhale.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Knees drifting far past the toes or inward.
  • Rounding the back.
