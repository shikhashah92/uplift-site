Plank

A core exercise using your body weight. The plank mainly works your abs, with your obliques helping.

  • abs
  • obliques

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Forearms under the shoulders, elbows about 90 degrees.
  • Feet together or hip-width; body in one straight line.

How to do it

  1. Brace the abs as if about to be punched; squeeze the glutes.
  2. Tuck the ribs down and breathe normally; hold for time.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Hips sagging toward the floor or piking up.
  • Head dropping / neck craning.
  • Holding your breath.
