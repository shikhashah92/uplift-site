Dumbbell Hammer Curl

A arms exercise with dumbbells. The dumbbell hammer curl mainly works your biceps and forearms.

  • biceps
  • forearms

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Neutral grip, palms facing in, elbows at the sides, tall posture.

How to do it

  1. Curl straight up keeping the palms neutral; squeeze.
  2. Lower slowly; alternate arms or both together.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Rocking the body for momentum.
  • Letting the elbows travel forward.
  • Rotating into a normal supinated curl.

Muscles worked

The dumbbell hammer curl mainly works your biceps and forearms.
