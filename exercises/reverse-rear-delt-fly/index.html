Reverse / Rear-Delt Fly

A shoulders exercise with dumbbells. The reverse / rear-delt fly mainly works your shoulders, with your upper back and traps helping.

  • shoulders
  • upper back
  • traps

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Hinge forward with a flat back, or rest the chest on an incline pad.
  • Soft elbows, weights hanging down, traps relaxed.

How to do it

  1. Open the arms out and back in a wide arc to shoulder level.
  2. Squeeze the rear delts; lower slowly.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Rowing with sharply bent elbows.
  • Using the lower back to swing the weight up.
  • Shrugging the traps toward the ears.
