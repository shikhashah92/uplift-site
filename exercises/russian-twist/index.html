Russian Twist

A core exercise using your body weight. The russian twist mainly works your obliques, with your abs helping.

  • obliques
  • abs

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Sit with knees bent, heels down or feet raised.
  • Lean the torso back ~45 degrees with a flat, not rounded, back; hands or a plate at the chest.

How to do it

  1. Rotate the shoulders and ribcage to one side, reach or tap.
  2. Rotate to the other side; move from the trunk, not just the arms.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Rounding the lower back.
  • Only swinging the arms while the torso stays still.
  • Rushing until it becomes flailing.

Muscles worked

The russian twist mainly works your obliques, with your abs helping.
