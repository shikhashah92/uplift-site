Rope Triceps Pushdown

A arms exercise on a cable machine. The rope triceps pushdown mainly works your triceps.

  • triceps

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Rope attachment on a high pulley, elbows pinned to the sides, slight forward lean.

How to do it

  1. Push down and apart, spreading the rope ends at the bottom and turning the palms in.
  2. Squeeze, return under control.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Elbows drifting away from the body.
  • Leaning on the weight instead of the triceps.
  • Letting the rope snap back up.
