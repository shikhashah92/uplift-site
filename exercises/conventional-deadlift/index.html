Conventional Deadlift

A back exercise with a barbell. The conventional deadlift mainly works your lower back, hamstrings and glutes, with your upper back, traps, forearms and quads helping.

  • lower back
  • hamstrings
  • glutes
  • upper back
  • traps
  • forearms

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Bar over mid-foot, shins about 2 cm away, hip-width stance.
  • Hinge and grip just outside the knees; chest up, lats tight, flat back.
  • Take the slack out of the bar before you pull.

How to do it

  1. Push the floor away; keep the bar dragging up the legs.
  2. Hips and shoulders rise together; stand tall and lock the hips.
  3. Hinge back down under control, bar close to the legs.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Rounding the lower back or jerking the bar off the floor.
  • Hips shooting up first (turns it into a stiff-leg pull).
  • Leaning back / hyperextending at the top.
