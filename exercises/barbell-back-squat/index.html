Barbell Back Squat

A legs exercise with a barbell. The barbell back squat mainly works your quads and glutes, with your inner thighs, hamstrings and lower back helping.

  • quads
  • glutes
  • inner thighs
  • hamstrings
  • lower back

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Bar on the upper traps or rear delts, hands snug.
  • Feet about shoulder-width, toes slightly out, whole foot planted.
  • Take a big breath and brace the trunk hard before you descend.

How to do it

  1. Break at the hips and knees together, knees tracking over the toes.
  2. Descend to at least parallel with a neutral spine.
  3. Drive up through the mid-foot, hips and chest rising together.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Knees caving inward.
  • Heels lifting / weight shifting onto the toes.
  • Lower back rounding at the bottom ('butt wink').
