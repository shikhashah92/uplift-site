Sun salutation (Surya Namaskar)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Getting into position

  • Stand at the front of the mat, feet together, palms joined at the chest.

How to do it

  1. Flow through the 12 positions: raise arms, fold forward, step back to lunge, plank, lower (eight limbs), cobra, downward dog, lunge, fold, rise, return.
  2. Inhale as you open or lift, exhale as you fold or lower.
  3. One round = both sides (right leg leads, then left).

Mistakes to avoid

  • Rushing and losing the breath.
  • Dropping the hips or collapsing the lower back in plank and cobra.
