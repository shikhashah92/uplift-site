Warrior I (Virabhadrasana I)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Getting into position

  • From standing, step the left foot back about a metre; turn it out slightly, heel down.

How to do it

  1. Bend the front knee over the ankle; hips square to the front.
  2. Raise the arms overhead.
  3. Hold, then do the other side: log each side as a round.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Front knee caving in.
  • Arching hard through the lower back.
