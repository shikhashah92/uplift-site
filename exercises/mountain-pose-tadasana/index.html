Mountain pose (Tadasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Getting into position

  • Feet together or hip-width, weight even across both feet.

How to do it

  1. Lengthen up through the crown; shoulders relaxed down.
  2. Optionally raise the arms overhead, palms facing, and stretch up (Urdhva Hastasana).
  3. Breathe slowly through the nose.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Locking the knees.
  • Pushing the ribs forward and arching the lower back.
