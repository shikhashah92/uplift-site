Half spinal twist (Ardha Matsyendrasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Getting into position

  • Sit tall; bend the right knee and step the right foot outside the left knee.

How to do it

  1. Right hand behind you; hug the knee with the left arm (or hook the elbow outside it).
  2. Inhale to lengthen, exhale to twist to the right.
  3. Hold, then do the other side: log each side as a round.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Twisting by pulling hard with the arms.
  • Slumping the spine.
