Hanging Leg Raise

A core exercise using your body weight. The hanging leg raise mainly works your abs, with your obliques and forearms helping.

  • abs
  • obliques
  • forearms

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Dead hang from the bar, shoulders lightly 'set', legs together.
  • Start with a slight backward tilt of the pelvis.

How to do it

  1. Curl the pelvis and lift the legs to at least parallel (or knees to chest).
  2. Lower slowly with no swing.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Kipping / swinging to build momentum.
  • Only raising the knees with a flat pelvis (hip-flexor-only).
  • Dropping the legs fast at the end.

Muscles worked

The hanging leg raise mainly works your abs, with your obliques and forearms helping.
