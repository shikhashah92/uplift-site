Fish pose (Matsyasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Getting into position

  • Lie on the back, legs straight; hands under the hips, palms down.

How to do it

  1. Press the forearms down and lift the chest.
  2. Lightly rest the crown of the head on the mat (little weight on it).
  3. A counter-pose to shoulder stand.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Putting body weight onto the head.
