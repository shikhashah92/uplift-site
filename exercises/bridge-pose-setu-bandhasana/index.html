Bridge pose (Setu Bandhasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Getting into position

  • Lie on the back, knees bent, feet hip-width near the hips; arms by the sides.

How to do it

  1. Press through the feet and lift the hips.
  2. Roll the shoulders under; clasp the hands beneath you if you like.
  3. Lower slowly, one vertebra at a time.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Knees falling apart.
  • Turning the head while up.
