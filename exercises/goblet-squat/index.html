Goblet Squat

A legs exercise with dumbbells. The goblet squat mainly works your quads and glutes, with your abs helping.

  • quads
  • glutes
  • abs

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Hold one dumbbell vertically at the chest, both hands cupping the top plate.
  • Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.

How to do it

  1. Squat down between the knees, elbows brushing the inside of the knees near the bottom.
  2. Drive up through the whole foot, keeping the chest tall.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Letting the dumbbell drift away from the chest.
  • Knees caving in.
  • Rounding the upper back to keep the weight close.
