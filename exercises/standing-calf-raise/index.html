Standing Calf Raise

A legs exercise on a machine. The standing calf raise mainly works your calves.

  • calves

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Balls of the feet on the platform, heels free to drop.
  • Knees straight but not locked, torso tall.

How to do it

  1. Rise onto the toes as high as possible; pause at the top.
  2. Lower slowly to a deep stretch below the platform.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Bouncing through a tiny range.
  • Bending the knees to cheat the movement.
  • No pause at the top or stretch at the bottom.
