Lying twist (Supta Matsyendrasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Getting into position

  • Lie on the back; hug the right knee in.

How to do it

  1. Let the right knee fall across the body to the left; right arm out to the side.
  2. Look to the right if the neck is happy.
  3. Hold, then do the other side: log each side as a round.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Forcing the knee to the floor.
