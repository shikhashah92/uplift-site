Cable Triceps Pushdown

A arms exercise on a cable machine. The cable triceps pushdown mainly works your triceps.

  • triceps

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • High pulley, bar attachment, elbows pinned to the sides, slight forward lean, core braced.

How to do it

  1. Extend the arms fully down.
  2. Squeeze the triceps; return under control to about 90 degrees.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Elbows drifting out or up.
  • Leaning over and using body weight to push.
  • Short reps that never lock out.
