Dhyana (Meditation)

A pranayama breathing practice. Here’s how to sit for it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Getting into position

  • Sit comfortably with the spine upright, eyes closed.

How to do it

  1. Rest attention on the breath (or a word or sound).
  2. When the mind wanders, notice and come back.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Judging yourself for wandering.

More poses

  • Anulom Vilom (Nadi Shodhana)
  • Kapalbhati (Skull-shining breath)
  • Bhastrika (Bellows breath)
  • Bhramari (Humming bee breath)
  • Ujjayi (Victorious breath)
  • Sheetali (Cooling breath)
