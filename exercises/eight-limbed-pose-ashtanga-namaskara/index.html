Eight-limbed pose (Ashtanga Namaskara)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Getting into position

  • From plank, lower the knees to the mat.

How to do it

  1. Lower the chest and chin between the hands, hips raised.
  2. Toes, knees, chest, chin and both hands touch the mat.
  3. Part of Surya Namaskar; hold briefly on its own.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Letting the elbows flare wide.
