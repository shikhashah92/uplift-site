Dumbbell Biceps Curl

A arms exercise with dumbbells. The dumbbell biceps curl mainly works your biceps, with your forearms helping.

  • biceps
  • forearms

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Stand tall, a dumbbell in each hand at the sides, elbows pinned close to the body.

How to do it

  1. Curl both dumbbells up together, rotating the palms up as they rise; squeeze, lower under control.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Swinging the torso for momentum.
  • Elbows drifting forward.
  • Cutting the bottom stretch short.

Muscles worked

The dumbbell biceps curl mainly works your biceps, with your forearms helping.
