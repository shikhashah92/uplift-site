EZ-Bar Skull Crushers

A arms exercise with a barbell. The ez-bar skull crushers mainly works your triceps.

  • triceps

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Lie on a flat bench, EZ-bar held with a narrow grip, upper arms vertical and fixed over the shoulders.

How to do it

  1. Lower the bar toward the forehead / just behind it by bending the elbows only.
  2. Extend back up without moving the upper arms.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Letting the elbows flare or drift back over the face.
  • Moving the upper arms instead of just the forearms.
  • Using so much weight the elbows get hammered.

Muscles worked

The ez-bar skull crushers mainly works your triceps.
