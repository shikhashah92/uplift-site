Straight-Arm Pulldown

A back exercise on a cable machine. The straight-arm pulldown mainly works your upper back, with your triceps helping.

  • upper back
  • triceps

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • High pulley; stand tall then hinge slightly forward.
  • Arms nearly straight (soft elbow), bar at about forehead height.

How to do it

  1. Sweep the bar down to the thighs in an arc using the lats.
  2. Keep the arms straight; return under control to the stretch.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Bending the elbows into a triceps pushdown.
  • Rounding the back as you pull.
  • Shrugging the shoulders.
