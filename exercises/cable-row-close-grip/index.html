Cable Row - Close Grip

A back exercise on a cable machine. The cable row - close grip mainly works your upper back, with your biceps helping.

  • upper back
  • biceps

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Set up

  • Close (V-bar) handle, seated tall with a natural spine, arms extended.

How to do it

  1. Pull the handle to the belly, elbows brushing the sides.
  2. Squeeze the mid-back; return to a controlled stretch.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Leaning far back to move more weight.
  • Shrugging up at the finish.
  • Letting the shoulders round forward on the stretch.
