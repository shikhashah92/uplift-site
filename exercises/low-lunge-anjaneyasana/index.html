Low lunge (Anjaneyasana)

A yoga pose. Here’s how to get into it, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid.

Drawings: Workout Guide / Everkinetic, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Getting into position

  • From downward dog step the right foot forward between the hands; lower the left knee to the mat.

How to do it

  1. Sink the hips forward and down until you feel the front of the left hip stretch.
  2. Lift the chest; hands on the front thigh or overhead.
  3. Hold, then do the other side: log each side as a round.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Front knee far past the ankle.
  • Knee pressing on a hard floor: pad it.
