How it works

This uses the Epley formula: 1RM = weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). Lift 100 kg for 5 reps and your estimated max is 100 × (1 + 5 ÷ 30) ≈ 116.7 kg. Uplift uses the same formula to track your best estimated 1RM for every lift.

Estimates are closest from heavy sets of 3–6 reps and get rough past 10. They also assume the set was close to your limit: if you had several reps left, your real max is higher.

Using the percentages

  • 85–95%: heavy sets of 1–5 reps, for strength.
  • 70–85%: sets of 6–12 reps, the usual range for building muscle.
  • 60–70%: lighter sets of 12–20 reps, and warm-ups.

Is it safe to test a real one-rep max?

For most people training to get fitter, there’s no need. An estimate from a hard set of 3–5 tells you nearly as much, with less risk. If you do test one, warm up well and use a spotter or safety bars.
