On each side

How to load a barbell

  1. Take the weight of the bar off the total: a standard Olympic bar is 20 kg (45 lb), and women’s and technique bars are often 15 kg or 10 kg.
  2. Halve what’s left. That’s the weight for each side.
  3. Make it up with the heaviest plates first, then the smaller ones on the outside. Use collars.

Example: 100 kg on a 20 kg bar leaves 80 kg, so 40 kg a side: a 25 and a 15. No 15s at your gym? A 25, a 10 and a 5 makes the same.

Which plates does it use?

Standard sets: 25, 20, 15, 10, 5, 2.5 and 1.25 kg plates, or 45, 35, 25, 10, 5 and 2.5 lb. If a total can’t be made exactly, it tells you the nearest weight you can load.
