Moving from Strong

Bring your Strong history with you

Years of sets don’t have to stay behind. Export them from Strong, import them into Uplift, and pick up where you left off. It takes about two minutes, and everything stays on your phone.

How to move

  1. Export from StrongIn the Strong app, open Settings → Export data. It saves a CSV file to your phone.
  2. Open UpliftGo to app.getuplift.pro in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, and add it to your Home Screen.
  3. Import the fileIn Uplift, open Me → Settings → Move from another app → Import a Strong CSV export, and pick the CSV. Uplift shows what it found before anything changes.

What comes across

  • Every workout, with its name, start time and how long it took
  • Every set: weight and reps, plus warm-up and drop sets
  • Cardio with distance and time, and timed holds like planks
  • Common Strong exercises link to Uplift’s own, with form cues and drawings. The rest become your own custom exercises.
  • Each workout name becomes a template, so your usual routines are ready to start
  • Kilograms or pounds: newer exports say which, and for older ones Uplift asks

Good to know

Set notes and RPE from Strong aren’t imported yet.

Importing again is safe. Workouts from the days in the file are replaced rather than doubled, and your data before the import is kept in Undo history.

The file is read on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and Uplift has no account or server to send it to.
