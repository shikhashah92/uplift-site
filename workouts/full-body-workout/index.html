Full body workout (A)

6 exercises that work your quads, glutes, chest, upper back, shoulders, hamstrings and abs. It takes about 45–60 minutes.

How to run it

  • Start each exercise with 3 sets. Pick a weight you can lift for about 8–12 reps with good form.
  • Rest 1–3 minutes between sets: longer for big lifts like squats, shorter for curls and raises.
  • When you reach the top of the rep range on every set, add a little weight next time. Uplift suggests that step for you, from what you did last time.
  • Warm up the first exercise with a couple of lighter sets.
